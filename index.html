About Niels Vejlyt

Niels Vejlyt is a Danish guitarist, composer, producer, and music educator. Having taught guitar since the early 1990s and professionally since the late 1990s, Niels has coached conservatory-bound musicians and professional guitarists across the globe.

With 13 albums, 8 books, and over 40 video programs, Niels's methodology combines rigorous physical speed mechanics with neo-classical composition and practical music theory.
